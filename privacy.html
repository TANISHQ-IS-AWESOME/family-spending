Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what Tilly ("we," "us") collects when you use the Tilly website and apps, why we collect it, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • Your receipts are yours. We use them only to run Tilly for you.
  • Receipt photos are read on your device and never uploaded.
  • No ads, no selling your data, and no third-party tracking or analytics.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes by our sign-in provider, Supabase Auth. If you create a recovery code, we store only a one-way hash of it.
  • What you add. Profiles (the names and colors you choose), receipts (store, date, items, prices, categories and tax), subscriptions, budgets and savings goals.
  • Pro status. Whether you have Pro, how you got it (trial, code or subscription) and when it ends. If you subscribe, Stripe gives us a customer number, a subscription number and the subscription's status. Stripe handles your payment details. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Support messages. What you send through Contact support, its topic, the reply email you give us, and basic app details (the app version and whether you use the web, Android or iPhone version).
  • Technical information. When you use Tilly, our hosting providers process standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, to deliver the service and keep it secure. Some of this is kept briefly in their logs.

We don't collect receipt photos, your location, your contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics and tracking data.

2. Where your data is kept

  • In your account, in our database at Supabase. Database rules make sure each account can read and change only its own data.
  • On your device. Tilly keeps a copy of your account's data, your preferences (like theme and currency) and your sign-in in your browser's or app's storage, so it opens quickly and works offline. Signing out removes the copy of your data from that device.

3. How we use it

  • To run Tilly: sign you in, save and sync your data, and work out your totals, insights, budgets and savings tips. Savings tips are calculated from your own data inside the app.
  • To provide Tilly Pro and handle trials, codes and subscriptions.
  • To answer support messages and fix problems.
  • To keep Tilly secure and prevent abuse.
  • To send emails you ask for, like password-reset links, and important notices about the service or our policies.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell or rent it, and we don't share it with data brokers.

4. Companies that help us run Tilly

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Tilly website files and receives standard request information when you load the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts in the app, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load.
  • Stripe processes payments if you subscribe to Pro.
  • Exchange-rate services (frankfurter.dev and open.er-api.com). Only if you pick a currency other than US dollars, the app asks them for current rates. These requests contain no account data, but the services receive your IP address.
  • Our email provider sends password-reset and account emails to your address.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or when it's needed to protect someone's safety, rights or property. If Tilly is ever reorganized or sold, your information may move to the new owner, who must keep following this policy or ask you before using your data in a different way.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account (Settings, then Account, then Delete account) permanently deletes your account, profiles, receipts, subscriptions, budgets, goals, Pro status and support messages from our database. Copies can remain in backups for a short time until they are replaced. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your data: all of it is in the app.
  • Export it: Settings, then Your data, gives you a spreadsheet of every receipt or a full backup of your account.
  • Delete single items, or your whole account, in the app.

Depending on where you live, for example California or the European Union and United Kingdom, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to some uses of it. You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, contact us. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. In the EU and UK, we process your data to provide the service you asked for, to keep it secure, and, where needed, with your consent.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules keep each account's data separate. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your recovery code private.

8. Children

Tilly isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it. Teens from 13 to 17 should use Tilly with a parent's or guardian's permission.

9. International users

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: in the app, go to Settings, then Help & support, then Contact support, and choose "Privacy or my data."
