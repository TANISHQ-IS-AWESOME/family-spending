    Help

    How do I add a receipt?

    Tap the + button, pick whose receipt it is, then take or choose a photo. Tilly reads the items and prices for you. Check each line, fix anything it misread, and tap Add. You can also type the items yourself.

    Are my receipt photos uploaded?

    No. Tilly reads receipts on your device. The photo never leaves it. Only the items you choose to add are saved to your account.

    The scanner got something wrong

    Scanning works best with a flat, well-lit receipt and the whole thing in the frame. You can fix any line before adding it, and change a saved receipt later: open Receipts, tap Edit, then Edit receipt.

    What is a profile?

    A profile is anyone or anything you want a separate total for: you, a partner, the kids, the household or a side business. Free accounts can have 3 profiles. Pro has no limit.

    How do budgets work?

    Set a monthly amount in Plan, then Budgets. Tilly shows what's left, how much you can spend each day to stay on track, and warns you when you get close. Budgets start over on the 1st of each month. The overall budget is free. Budgets for each category and person come with Pro.

    How does Tilly find ways to save?

    Tilly looks at your last few months of receipts for fees, subscriptions, categories running higher than usual and stores that charge more for the same item. Each tip says how its saving was worked out. Savings are estimates, not promises.

    How do subscriptions work?

    Add a subscription in Plan, then Subscriptions, with its price and how often it renews. Tilly adds each charge to Receipts on its date and shows what's coming up.

    What comes with Tilly Pro?

    The cheaper-store finder, price-rise alerts and price tracker, budgets for every category and person, unlimited profiles and goals, monthly PDF reports and Pro colors. You can try it free for 14 days without a card.

    How do I cancel Pro?

    If you pay for Pro, go to Settings, then Tilly Pro, then Manage subscription, and cancel any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. The free trial ends by itself and never charges you.

    Does Tilly work offline?

    Yes. Your data is kept on your device too, so you can look at it and add receipts without a connection. Changes sync when you're back online.

    How do I get my data out?

    In Settings, under Your data, you can download every receipt as a spreadsheet file or a full backup of your account.

    I forgot my password

    On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? to get a reset link by email, or use the recovery code you saved when you made your account.

    How do I delete my account?

    Settings, then Account, then Delete account. It removes your account and everything in it for good.
